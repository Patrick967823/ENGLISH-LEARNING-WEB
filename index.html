📖 Reading Story: The Beauty of Thai Culture and Smiles

Thailand is known worldwide as "The Land of Smiles." Thai people are famous for their kindness, gentle nature, and beautiful culture. From elegant traditional dances and colorful festivals to magnificent temples with golden roofs, Thai culture is full of beauty and warmth.

The Thai smile is very special. It shows respect, happiness, and a peaceful heart. Whether you meet someone in a busy market or on a quiet beach, a warm Thai smile always makes you feel welcome.

Comprehension Questions:

1. What is Thailand called in the story?
2. What does the Thai smile represent?
3. What are two examples of Thai culture mentioned?
